## Patterns applied across all 4 docs

1. **P2 — Inverted Pyramid:** Every doc now leads with what the thing IS, not why it exists. Problem/motivation moved after the answer in every case.
2. **P9 — Concrete aid in first 2 screenfuls:** Every doc now has a table within the first screenful.
3. **P13 — Conciseness:** 30% word reduction across all docs. Every removed word was either restated context, transitional filler, or redundant explanation.
4. **P15 — Actionable Exit:** Every doc now ends with a single-line "Next:" format with a specific link and description.
5. **P3 — Heading Quality:** All vague headings ("The problem", "The answer", "The roles") replaced with standalone information-scent signals.
6. **P4 — Cognitive Load:** Large sections (5+ concepts) split into sub-sections with max 4 concepts each.
7. **P10 — Every key concept gets a concrete aid:** Added 8 new tables across the 4 docs. Key concepts (layers, roles, autonomy, renderers, absences, projections, differences) all have tables.
