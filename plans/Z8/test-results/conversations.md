## New Issues (not explicitly in the FLAGS list)

1. **List/sidebar count mismatch with reality.** The thread list placeholder titles (`"Agent thread fc836…"`, `"Task thread 2ca7aa6a"`) are pure UUID stubs with empty snippets — every row is visually indistinguishable except by host-kind prefix and timestamp. Combined with FLAG-1's broken sort, the inbox is effectively unusable for finding a conversation. (This is the aggregate UX consequence of FLAG-1 + FLAG-1.12/1.13; worth calling out as a single user-facing severity.)
2. **External (agent) replies are invisible until manual reload even on the thread you're actively reading** — FLAG-5 covers the mechanism, but the user-facing effect is stronger than "no live updates": during an active agent session a founder watching the thread sees a frozen view with zero indication new content exists. No polling fallback either.
3. **`net::ERR_ABORTED` on in-flight fetches during navigation** — 8 aborted requests observed (`/api/threads/assistant`, `/api/threads/:id`, `/api/recipients`) when navigating/remounting quickly. These are benign aborts (React effect cleanup) and produced **0 console errors and 0 non-2xx responses**, but they indicate fetches are not consistently tied to AbortControllers tracked by the UI; harmless today, noise for debugging.
4. **Questionnaire promotion steals the verdict toolbar.** When a thread has both an unresolved questionnaire and an unresolved review, `promotedRichMessageId` promotes only one (the questionnaire here), so the review's verdict toolbar never renders — the founder cannot act on the review until the questionnaire is resolved. Combined with FLAG-10 (questionnaire options are dead), the thread can reach a state where neither the questionnaire nor the review is actionable from the promoted card. Observed in `10-rich-cards.png` (review rendered as inert inline CommentRow, no toolbar).
