But a task reaching `done` does not end its session: `task-kill-cascade.ts:105-117` says so outright ("nothing ends a session when its task moves to `done`: the transition writes task state and `completedAt`, and the `done` handler only propagates UPWARD"). ## Verdict — `architecture-no-teardown.md`

**Build with the listed fixes, after B1 and B2 are answered.** The shape is right: moving the worktree to
`<keptCopiesRoot>/worktrees/<taskKey>/` and symlinking it in is the only option that survives both a
non-resume second session and the 24-hour reaper, it reuses a shape already proven at G2, and the four
reasons given for it hold against the code. The deletions are honest and the door is the smallest way to
carry the trigger. What blocks is the trigger's timing: nothing in the system ends a session when its task
turns `done`, so as written the release runs under a live harness (B1), and `--force` makes that a
destructive act on work the agent never committed (B2). Both are design questions inside §4, not
redesigns of §3. S2 is the one place the document goes past its own ruling — it deletes the session
scratch delete that its own §2 argues is harmless — and S3 is the one place it stops short of the gate it
has to pass. Fix S1 and S4-S6 in the text; they are omissions, not errors of direction.

## Verdict — `architecture-phase2.md` (revised)

**Build as is, with S7.** The revision does what it claims: one kept copy per Drive filesystem at
`<keptCopiesRoot>/<filesystemId>`, refreshed before each session, symlinked into the workspace, removed
only by `DELETE /api/filesystems/:id`. I grepped for surviving per-session or teardown language and found
none beyond one stale phrase (N-g). The adapter is a faithful twin of the remote-git kept copy, the
`resourceRef.kind` selection is the right axis, `copy` over `sync`/`bisync` and `--drive-skip-gdocs` are
both argued from rclone's own documentation, and the reason strings name a fix rather than guessing at
rclone's English. The one open item is now closed: `--update` exists and behaves as §2.2 assumes, so the
UNVERIFIED block and its fallback should be replaced by the citation — together with the honest statement
that a Drive-side edit newer than the agent's local edit still wins. Note the dependency is real: this
document rides `trustedPaths`, the symlink recognition and the absence of `unmount`, so it cannot land
before the no-teardown change does. Option A over B, for the reason the document gives.
