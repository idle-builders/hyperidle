# Wave 1 — Daily Loop Journeys

Discovery lens: the daily-driver loop. What founders (CEO + CPO) do every day or every few days to keep the Product Factory running. This is pure JTBD discovery from product intent — no app inspection, no UI fixes.

Source intent: HUMAN_INTERFACE_LAYER.md §5 (the day/week/month journey), REQUIREMENTS.md §1.1 (the five interaction modes — Review, Prioritize, Bet, Coach, Curate), §2.2–2.4 (notifications, the Rail), PROCESS_LAYER.md (Bets, Tasks, Sessions).

Founder personas are mostly symmetric on the daily loop — both touch Conversations, both glance at the Rail. They diverge mainly in routing (CEO ← market/growth/portfolio; CPO ← product/design/tech/content per F-6.4). Where the JTBD is identical, "Founder" is used; where it differs, CEO and CPO are split.

## Open questions

Journeys I suspect should exist in the daily loop but couldn't confirm from REQUIREMENTS.md / HUMAN_INTERFACE_LAYER.md / PROCESS_LAYER.md:

1. **Self-routing / "send this to my partner founder."** F-6.4 implies CoS routes per-founder, but is there an explicit human reassign action when CoS gets it wrong? Worth confirming whether the founder needs an affordance or if they just reply "this is for the CPO."
2. **Searching across threads / artifacts mid-day.** ⌘K global search exists (UX-8.1) — but is daily search a real JTBD or just an as-needed affordance? Probably an affordance; not listed as a journey.
3. **Reading agent-to-agent threads I'm copied on.** Mentioned in §3.3 ("threads the human is copied on") but no clear journey for "I'm cc'd, do I need to act?" — should the human ever read these proactively, or only when an agent escalates?
4. **Resuming a snoozed thread.** Implicit in DL-08 but the explicit "snoozed items came back" journey is unclear — is there a notification when a snooze fires, or does the user just see it again on next open?
5. **Catching a deadline that's about to slip.** F-2 mentions optional `deadline` on Items. Is there a daily "what's about to slip" surface, or is this the PM facilitator's job and it lands in Conversations only when material?
6. **Manually triggering an autonomous L4 routine** (e.g., "run the cost retro now"). Could be supporting; unclear whether it's part of the daily loop or only Settings.
7. **Reading the audit trail of a finished item to understand what happened.** Mentioned in F-2 (audit_trail field) and DL-20 — is this a daily JTBD or an exception path? Treated as supporting in DL-20.
8. **Verifying agents picked up a knowledge change I made yesterday.** Likely a Knowledge-view journey, not daily — defer to weekly/curate wave.

## Report

Discovered: 31 journeys (29 founder-symmetric + 2 split CEO/CPO).

Top 3 by value (most central to keeping the factory running daily):
1. **DL-02 Triage overnight blocking reviews** — the literal definition of "human in the loops that matter." Without this loop, paused agents stall.
2. **DL-09 Notice + unblock a stuck agent** — the Rail → Conversations bridge. The whole point of ambient awareness on the right sidebar.
3. **DL-16 Reply to the Chief of Staff** — the only cross-product judgment surface. Easy to skip, highest leverage; the pinned thread is the entire trust mechanism for systemic improvement.

Surprising patterns:
- **Mobile carries serious weight in the daily loop** (DL-26, DL-27, DL-28). The product intent is "morning + evening on phone, workday on desktop." Three of the daily-core journeys are phone-first, which means mobile is not a "companion" in the dismissive sense — it's load-bearing for the daily rhythm.
- **Anomaly journeys (DL-29, DL-30, DL-31)** are rare individually but high-value. They justify Rail features (burn-rate, system stability) that look like dashboard-noise on a calm day. Easy to under-design these because they don't show up in normal-day usage.
- **CEO/CPO split is thinner than expected** for the daily loop. The split shows up in routing (per-domain queues), not in distinct flows. Most JTBDs are symmetric; both founders need the same affordances.
- **DL-20 (overnight L4 work review)** is implicit in the L1–L4 autonomy model but not explicitly called out as a journey in the docs. If autonomy is supposed to grow, the founder needs a low-friction way to spot-check what happened while they were absent — otherwise trust never compounds.
