Director's cut · Rev 01

Knowledge, reconciled.

Version A is a nearly-complete visual proposal — a scope browser, topic-grouped lists, a Triage view, a merge modal, detail with observation logs. Version B is the shipping React app in web/src — KnowledgeList, KnowledgeDetail, KnowledgeRow, plus the Submenu, Tag, StatusChip, and KeyValue primitives — but structurally the thinnest surface in the codebase: a flat row list, a 2-KV detail page, a single "All Items" submenu entry.

Most of this synthesis is therefore [NEW] or [STRUCTURAL] — Version B is right to keep its primitives; it just hasn't composed them yet. Version A carries the composition. The job here is to lock those two together so the existing tests keep passing, the existing types stay canonical, and the missing surfaces (scope browser, triage, merge, factory weight, attention scoring) ship against real file paths.

What we kept, what we changed

From Version B (canonical): KnowledgeKind / KnowledgeLayer / KnowledgeStatus from @hyperidle/shared, the KNOWLEDGE_STATUS_MAP in status-mapping.ts (including needs_human → behind), the KnowledgeRow composition (title + layer Tag + kind Tag + StatusChip + timestamp), the Submenu contract (collapse, search, nav children), DaisyUI badge / alert / skeleton primitives. From Version A (IA & missing surfaces): the scope selector (Practice / Product / Factory), topic grouping inside the list, Triage vs Curated switching, curation suggestion cards, merge / split modals, knowledge-gap indicators, attention scoring, the Factory constitution treatment. New (agreed today): the Why now attention reason line on Triage rows, the observation-log in the detail gutter with facilitator-agent attribution, and the Factory scope's “amendable under review” banner.
