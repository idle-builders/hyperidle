# HyperIdle User Guide

HyperIdle puts you at the head of a flat team of humans and AI agents. You shape work as bets, agents execute, you review and redirect. Agents act by default — reviews and gates are chosen by you or earned from real outcomes, not imposed on every action. Every session, decision, and pattern is captured and reused: knowledge compounds across your team over time.
