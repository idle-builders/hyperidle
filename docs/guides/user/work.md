# Work

Work is the tasks agents do. An agent owns each task; you never do. What a task needs from you — an answer or a review — reaches you in Conversations. The Work list shows top-level tasks only; open one to see its page.

---

## The task page

From top to bottom:

1. **Title, where it stands, and what it is about.**
2. **Parts** — the task's parts drawn as a graph.
3. **Decisions and assumptions.**
4. **Done when.**
5. **The owner's summary.**
6. **The conversation** of the task or part you are looking at.

A box on the right holds everything attached and the things you can do.

### Parts

Parts run left to right, each with its subtasks to its right. A dashed arrow points from a part to the part that waits for it. Each part has a mark for how it stands — ✓ done, ◐ under way, ○ not started, ■ stopped, or the initials of the agent working on it now — then its name and a few words.

Where a person is needed, the part shows an icon: **?** needs input, an **eye** needs a review. A part with subtasks counts the icons below it. A long row of finished parts folds into one "N earlier, done" chip.

Click a part to look at it: the page and the box switch to that part, and its address keeps your place. **The whole task** above the graph goes back.

### Decisions and assumptions

What the work rests on. A **decision** says who made it and where; one that waits for an answer shows the **?**. An **assumption** is marked ✓ held, ○ not checked yet, or ✗ did not hold, with how it was or will be checked.

### Done when

Each line says what has to be true, and how it closes: **Checks itself** (with its evidence) or **A person reviews**.

### Summary and conversation

The owner writes the summary: its notes while it runs, the outcome when it is done. Below it is the conversation. To steer the work, write there; the agent reads it.

## The box

- **Follow** / **Following** — be told when this task changes (see [Notifications](./the-interface.md#notifications-the-bell)).
- **Put on hold** — the agents finish what they are doing, then wait. **Take off hold** lets them go on.
- **Stop** — the agents stop where they are; nothing they did is undone. **Start again** picks up from there.
- **Ask for a review** — adds a "Done when" line, "A person looked at it before it counts as done", so the task cannot close without a review.
- **Facts** — who owns it, when it started or finished, time spent in sessions, and the thread it came from.
- **Questions**, **Reviews** and **Documents**, each in its own section. A row opens its question, review or document; one that sits on another part says which. Documents made while working on the parts are folded under "Show N more".
- **Sessions** — the newest run; earlier ones open in place. **Peek** shows a running session's steps.
