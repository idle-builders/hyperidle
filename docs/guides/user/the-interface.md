# The interface

The app has one sidebar on the left and the page you opened on the right. There are no tabs, no home screen and no settings screen. Every page has its own address, so back, forward and reload keep your place.

---

## The sidebar, top to bottom

1. **Find or ask… ⌘K** and the **bell**.
2. **Conversations · Work · Knowledge**, stacked. Conversations and Knowledge show a count of what waits on you. Work never does: agents own the work, and what they need from you shows up in Conversations. Picking one opens the page you last had open there, or its first item the first time.
3. A line, then the list for the one you picked.
4. **You · N waiting for you** at the bottom. It opens [your page](./your-page.md).

### The lists

Each list is in order of what matters to you: what you owe first, then what is new, then the rest. Each row starts with a small mark for its status; point at it to read the status in words.

- **Conversations** — **+ New conversation**, then every thread. A row shows its mark, the title and time, then who. Threads that wait on you are marked: **?** needs input or is stuck (with who asks), an **eye** needs your review, **@** mentioned you (until you open it). Other rows say **New reply** or show the last message.
- **Work** — a team switch (**All teams**, or one team with its number of tasks). Once you pick a team, **The team ›** opens [the team's page](./running-your-team.md). Below it are the top-level tasks, each marked the way its graph is: ○ queued, ◐ under way, ✓ done, ■ stopped, **?** or an **eye** where you are needed. Point at the mark for the words ("3 of 7 done", On hold).
- **Knowledge** — first, drafts someone else wrote (marked with an **eye**, **Publish?**) and documents edited since you looked. Then the document tree: one space per team, documents nested inside. Click a document's icon to change it; the **+** on a row adds a new document under it.

While you edit a document, the conversation list holds still and says "N new since you started · it waits here", so nothing jumps under you.

## Find or ask (⌘K)

One box, two uses. **Tab** switches between them and keeps what you typed.

- **Find** lists matching documents, conversations and tasks, with the matching text shown beside the list. Use ↑↓ to move and ↵ to open.
- **Ask** sends your question to an agent. By default that is the agent you have had longest; pick another under "Ask" before you send. The answer appears in the box as the agent writes it, with its sources. The question is saved as a conversation: ↵ asks a follow-up, ⌘↵ opens the thread.

**esc** or a click outside closes it.

---

## Notifications (the bell)

The bell shows how many notifications are new. It opens a panel of what happened in things you follow: a task done, an agent stuck, a document published or edited, a new reply. Click one to open the thread, task or document. Opening that thread, task or document any other way also reads its notifications. **Mark all read** clears the count.

You get these for what you follow. You follow a task or a published document with **Follow** on its page; **Change what you are told about** at the bottom of the panel opens the switches on [your page](./your-page.md).

---

## When a page does not load

If something fails to load, the page says so in one line with **Try again**.
