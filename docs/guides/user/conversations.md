# Conversations

Every thread lives in Conversations: talks you start, questions you ask in Find or ask, the comments on a document, and the reviews agents raise. People and agents take part the same way.

---

## A thread

A thread shows its messages, newest at the bottom, and opens at the newest one. Write in the box at the bottom: ↵ sends, shift-↵ starts a new line.

Under the box is the format bar: **Mention · Link · Bold · Italic · List · Quote · Code**. Each button adds that piece where you are writing, so you never need to know the typing shortcuts.

Typing `@` opens the picker: people and agents in one list, each with what they do and how busy they are. ↑↓ choose, ↵ picks, esc closes it and keeps what you typed.

## Starting one

**+ New conversation** at the top of the Conversations list. Say what it is about and `@mention` who should be in it, people and agents alike. You must mention at least one. The first line becomes the title.

To talk with one person or agent directly, use **Open conversation** on their page.

---

## Handing work to an agent

Mention an agent with `@` and say what you need. The agent starts a task for it. The task keeps this thread as its own and says where it came from.

The task shows in the thread as one line: its title, who owns it, how it stands (Queued, Running, Needs input, Needs review, Done…) and **Open ›**, which opens [the task page](./work.md). The line changes in place as the task moves, so the thread does not fill up with status messages.

While an agent works, one line says so: "Builder is working · 12m · 3 steps". **Peek at session** shows the steps; the agent's working chatter never enters the thread.

---

## Questions

When an agent needs your judgement it asks in the thread, with real options. A question the agent cannot go on without is marked **Stuck**; others say **Needs input**. Pick an option to answer; the card then shows what was chosen and by whom. To say something else, reply in the thread.

---

## Reviews

When an agent has a result for you to check, it raises a review in the thread. Yours is marked **Needs review** and has **Look at it**. Someone else's review shows in full with who it waits on.

**Look at it** opens the review in place of the thread. **Thread** at the top goes back.

### What a review shows

- What the agent says about the result, and what changed since the last round.
- **How this closes** and **Decisions and assumptions** from the task (see [Work](./work.md)).
- The work itself, in **parts**: a live page you can see at desktop, tablet and phone widths, a code change, an image, numbers, a piece of text such as an email, or a batch of items such as three replies to customers. **More space** gives a page, code or image more room.

### Pins and questions

The agent flags the spots it is unsure of with **pins**. Point at a spot to pin your own comment there: click something on a live page, a line of code, a figure or an item, select words in a text, or drag over part of an image. Every pin has its own small thread.

**Questions on this review** lists every pin — who asked, where, and whether it is Open, Answered or Settled — so you never have to hunt for a comment. **Anything overall?** at the bottom takes a comment on the whole thing.

### Approve or reject

- **Approve** — done. Open questions do not block it; their number sits beside the buttons.
- **Reject** — you must say why. Your reason goes back to the agent and the session that raised the review (**Reject and send**).

A retry is a new review, **round 2**. It links back to round 1 and says how many questions are still open there. Open questions stay on the round they were asked in.
